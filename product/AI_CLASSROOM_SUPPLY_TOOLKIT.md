     1|# AI Classroom Supply Donor Outreach + Teacher Wishlist Toolkit
     2|
     3|Version: 1.0
     4|Disclosure: This toolkit was created with AI assistance and human-supervised rules for lawful, honest fundraising communications.
     5|Purpose: Help teachers, parents, PTO/PTA volunteers, and community helpers organize school-supply support requests without misleading claims, spam, or pressure tactics.
     6|
     7|## What this toolkit helps you do
     8|
     9|- Turn a vague school-supply need into a clear, specific wishlist.
    10|- Write honest messages to local supporters without guilt-tripping or pretending to be someone else.
    11|- Track who has been contacted, who replied, and what supplies were pledged.
    12|- Send thank-you notes and impact updates.
    13|- Avoid common compliance mistakes such as fake urgency, fake nonprofit claims, and unsolicited spam blasts.
    14|
    15|## Important boundaries
    16|
    17|Use this only for real classroom/school/community needs you are authorized to discuss.
    18|Do not claim nonprofit status unless your organization actually has it.
    19|Do not message strangers in bulk.
    20|Do not use fake student stories, fake photos, fake receipts, or fake urgency.
    21|Do not publish student personal information.
    22|If your school/district has fundraising rules, follow them first.
    23|
    24|## Quick-start checklist
    25|
    26|1. Name the specific supply need.
    27|2. Estimate quantities and approximate cost.
    28|3. Confirm who is allowed to receive supplies/funds.
    29|4. Choose a legitimate wishlist/payment path approved by your school or group.
    30|5. Use one message template below.
    31|6. Track every response in the included tracker.
    32|7. Send thank-you notes and share only privacy-safe updates.
    33|
    34|## Template 1: Warm community message
    35|
    36|Hi [Name], I’m helping organize a small school-supply support effort for [class/group/school]. The current priority is [specific supplies], because [plain reason].
    37|
    38|If you already planned to help with school supplies this season, here’s the approved wishlist/info: [link or instructions]. No pressure either way — I’m just keeping the request specific and organized so nothing gets duplicated.
    39|
    40|Thank you for considering it.
    41|
    42|## Template 2: Local business sponsorship message
    43|
    44|Hello [Business Name], I’m organizing a small, specific school-supply support request for [class/group/school]. We’re looking for [specific items or sponsorship amount] to help cover [clear use].
    45|
    46|If your business supports local school needs, I can send a short wishlist and receipt/thank-you process. I’m keeping this transparent: no fake urgency, no broad donation pitch, just a clear supply need and follow-up confirmation.
    47|
    48|Would you like the details?
    49|
    50|## Template 3: PTO/PTA coordination message
    51|
    52|Hi everyone, to avoid duplicate purchases, I made a simple tracker for the current supply need: [need]. Please add only confirmed pledges or purchases.
    53|
    54|Priority items:
    55|- [Item 1]
    56|- [Item 2]
    57|- [Item 3]
    58|
    59|Privacy reminder: do not include student names or private family details in public messages.
    60|
    61|## Template 4: Thank-you note
    62|
    63|Thank you for helping with [specific supplies]. Your support helped cover [specific need] for [class/group/school].
    64|
    65|We appreciate it. I’ll keep updates privacy-safe and focused on the supplies received, not student personal details.
    66|
    67|## Template 5: No-pressure follow-up
    68|
    69|Hi [Name], just a quick follow-up on the school-supply request I sent. If it’s not a fit, no worries at all. If you do want to help, the current priority is still [specific item/link].
    70|
    71|Thanks either way.

    87|## Compliance self-check
    88|
    89|Before sending any message, confirm:
    90|
    91|- I am authorized to make this request.
    92|- The need is real and specific.
    93|- I am not pretending to be a school official/nonprofit if I am not one.
    94|- I am not using private student details.
    95|- I am not messaging strangers in bulk.
    96|- I can show where supplies/funds went.
