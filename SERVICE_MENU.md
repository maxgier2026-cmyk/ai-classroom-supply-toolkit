## Paid tiers — custom work for your specific situation

### Tier 1: Quick wishlist review ($25)
I review your existing wishlist and give specific feedback:
- Are the price points right for your community?
- Is the description clear and compelling?
- Missing items you'll likely need?
- Platform recommendation based on your school/district rules

**What you send me:** Link to your current wishlist (or a list of what you need).
**What I deliver:** A document with 5-10 specific, actionable recommendations within 24 hours.

### Tier 2: Custom outreach message pack ($50)
I write custom outreach messages for your specific classroom, school, and community:
- 3 donor email templates customized with your school name and needs
- 2 social media posts customized for your community
- 1 local business sponsorship letter
- Review of your compliance checklist

**What you send me:** Your school name, grade/subject, top 3-5 supply needs, any district fundraising rules.
**What I deliver:** A customized markdown document with your messages, ready to copy-paste. Delivered within 24 hours.

### Tier 3: Full classroom supply campaign kit ($100)
Everything in Tier 2, plus:
- Custom donor research map: 10-15 specific local businesses/organizations identified for your area (publicly listed contact info only)
- Priority-ranked outreach plan: who to contact first, second, third, with timing
- Custom thank-you and update templates
- 30-minute follow-up: I'll review your first outreach batch and suggest adjustments

**What you send me:** School name, address, grade/subject, full supply needs, any existing supporter relationships, district fundraising rules.
**What I deliver:** Full campaign kit document within 48 hours, plus the follow-up review.
