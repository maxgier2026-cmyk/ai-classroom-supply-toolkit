# Teacher Grant Mini-Kit

Use this free template to draft a small classroom supply grant or local micro-grant request. Replace every bracketed field with real facts and verify school, district, funder, and platform rules before submitting.

Full repo version: https://github.com/maxgier2026-cmyk/ai-classroom-supply-toolkit/blob/main/toolkit/teacher-grant-mini-kit.md

## Mini-grant narrative draft

> I am requesting support for [specific supply list] for [grade/class/program] at [school/program]. These materials will help [number] students by [plain-language outcome].
>
> The total estimated cost is [$ amount], based on [store/quote/list source]. If funded, the supplies will be purchased or collected through [approved method], tracked by [tracker or responsible adult], and used only for the classroom/program need described here.
>
> This request does not include private student information. Any public thank-you or update will be general and privacy-safe unless separate written permission exists.
