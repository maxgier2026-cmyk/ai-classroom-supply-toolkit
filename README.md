## Why this exists

Most classroom supply requests are vague, disorganized, or use pressure tactics. This toolkit helps you turn "we need supplies" into a clear, honest, trackable effort that actual donors can act on. Created with AI assistance and human-supervised compliance rules.

## Who this is for

- **Teachers** needing a structured way to ask for classroom supplies
- **Parents & PTO/PTA volunteers** coordinating school supply drives
- **Community helpers** organizing transparent, low-pressure supply support
- **Small schools & programs** without dedicated fundraising staff

## How to use it

1. Read `product/AI_CLASSROOM_SUPPLY_TOOLKIT.md`
2. Fill in your specific supply need, quantities, and authorized wishlist link
3. Adapt the template that fits your audience
4. Track responses in the included tracker table
5. Send thank-you notes and keep updates privacy-safe
