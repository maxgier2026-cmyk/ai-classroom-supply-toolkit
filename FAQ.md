# FAQ — AI Classroom Supply Toolkit

## General

### Is this really free?
The core toolkit templates are publicly available. Customization services (someone adapts the templates for your specific classroom/school) are paid, starting at $29.

### Who made this?
Created with AI assistance under human-supervised compliance rules as part of a charity income-generation experiment. All net proceeds go toward school supplies for children in need.

### Can I use this for any school?
Yes, as long as you are authorized to communicate about that school's supply needs. Adapt messages to your actual role, school/district policies, and platform rules.

## For Teachers

### I'm a teacher. How do I ask for supplies without feeling awkward?
Start with the warm community message template (Template 1). It's direct, specific, and low-pressure — no guilt trips. Name the actual items needed, link an authorized wishlist, and make it easy for people to help.

### What's the difference between the free toolkit and the paid customization?
The free toolkit gives you editable templates and worksheets. The paid customization service ($29–$149) means I adapt the templates specifically to your classroom, supply list, and audience. You get ready-to-send messages, not templates you have to fill in yourself.

### Do I need principal/district approval?
Check your school/district policy before soliciting donations. The toolkit includes a compliance self-check to help you stay within rules.

## For Parents and PTO/PTA

### How is this different from a regular fundraiser?
No selling, no guilt, no minimums. It's a structured way to name specific classroom needs and make it easy for community members to help — whether through direct donation, wishlist purchase, or sharing the request.

### Can I use this for multiple classrooms?
Yes. The organization kit ($149) covers three audience-specific message sets. You can coordinate across grade levels or subjects.

### What about privacy?
Templates are designed to avoid sharing student names, photos, or personal details. Thank-you messages can be general ("the third-grade class") unless you have explicit parent permission for specifics.

## For Community Helpers

### I want to help local schools but don't know where to start.
The sponsorship request template (Template 2) is written for local businesses and community members who want to support schools. It asks for specific items, not cash, and gives a clear way to help.

### Can I share this toolkit with teachers I know?
Yes! The repo is public. Point them to the README or share the specific template that fits their situation.
