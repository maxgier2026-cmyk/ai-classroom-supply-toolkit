## Mini-grant narrative draft

> I am requesting support for [specific supply list] for [grade/class/program] at [school/program]. These materials will help [number] students by [plain-language outcome: replacing worn supplies, creating individual supply kits, supporting reading/math/science activities, preparing for back-to-school, etc.].
>
> The total estimated cost is [$ amount], based on [store/quote/list source]. If funded, the supplies will be purchased or collected through [approved method], tracked by [tracker or responsible adult], and used only for the classroom/program need described here.
>
> This request does not include private student information. Any public thank-you or update will be general and privacy-safe unless separate written permission exists.

## Short impact statement options

Pick one and edit with real facts:

1. **Back-to-school readiness:** These supplies will help students start the school year with the basic materials needed to participate from day one.
2. **Shared classroom resources:** These items will restock common classroom materials so students are not left out when family budgets are tight.
3. **Project support:** These supplies will support upcoming classroom activities in [subject/project] and reduce out-of-pocket costs for families and staff.
4. **Equity-focused:** The goal is to make essential supplies available in the classroom without calling attention to which students brought items from home.

## Compliance self-check before submitting

- [ ] I am authorized to request support for this classroom/program.
- [ ] The funder/platform rules allow this kind of request.
- [ ] The budget uses real prices or reasonable current estimates.
- [ ] I did not include student personal data.
- [ ] I did not imply nonprofit/tax status unless it is verified.
- [ ] I have a plan to track received items/funds and send a privacy-safe thank-you/update.

## Funder follow-up message

> Thank you for considering this classroom supply request. If funded, I will provide a brief privacy-safe update showing what was purchased/received and how the supplies support the class/program. I will not share student personal information in public updates.
