# Donor Email Sequence for Classroom Supply Drives

Status: REAL_DELIVERABLE_READY
Created: 2026-06-12T22:07:07Z
Use case: teachers, parents, PTO/PTA volunteers, and community helpers who are authorized to request support for real classroom or school supply needs.
AI disclosure: drafted with AI assistance and intended for human review before sending.

## Compliance guardrails

- Only send to people you have a legitimate reason to contact.
- Follow school, district, employer, platform, and fundraising rules.
- Do not include student names, student photos, private family details, or unsupported claims.
- Do not imply nonprofit/tax-deductible status unless it is actually true and documented.
- Do not pressure, shame, or repeatedly message people who do not respond.
- Replace every bracketed field with true information before use.

## Minimal facts to gather first

| Field | Example | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| School/program name | Lincoln Elementary art room | Yes |
| Role/authorization | 4th grade teacher / PTO supply drive coordinator | Yes |
| Supply goal | $300 in pencils, notebooks, tissues, and headphones | Yes |
| Wishlist or drop-off method | Official wishlist link or school office drop-off | Yes |
| Deadline | August 10 before back-to-school night | Optional |
| Public thank-you plan | Aggregate thank-you only, no student names | Recommended |

## Email 1 — warm local request

Subject: Classroom supply list for [grade/program]

Hi [Name],

I’m helping organize supplies for [classroom/program] at [school/program]. The current priority is simple, practical items: [top 3-5 items].

If you’re already looking for a small way to help local students this season, here is the wishlist/drop-off information: [authorized link or drop-off instructions].

No pressure at all — I’m sharing it clearly so people who want to help can see exactly what is needed. I’ll post a general thank-you/update after the drive without sharing student private information.

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Your role]

## Email 2 — local business sponsor request

Subject: Local classroom supply support request

Hi [Business/Owner Name],

I’m reaching out because [school/program] is preparing for [school year/event], and [grade/program] needs help with basic supplies like [items].

A small contribution such as [specific ask: e.g., 10 boxes of pencils, 20 notebooks, $50 in supplies] would directly support [number] students. Here is the authorized wishlist/drop-off method: [link/instructions].

If your business participates, I can provide a simple public thank-you using your business name only, if that is allowed by school/program rules. No student photos or private details will be used.

Would this be something you’re open to reviewing?

Thank you,
[Your name]
[Your role]

## Email 3 — PTO/PTA coordination request

Subject: Coordinating [grade/program] supply needs

Hi [Coordinator Name],

I’m organizing the supply request for [grade/program] and want to make sure it fits the PTO/PTA and school rules before sharing widely.

Current needs:
- [Item 1] — [quantity]
- [Item 2] — [quantity]
- [Item 3] — [quantity]

Proposed sharing method: [wishlist link / flyer / school newsletter / parent group post]
Proposed deadline: [date]
Privacy plan: no student names, no student photos, aggregate thank-you only.

Can you confirm whether this is okay to share, or tell me what needs to change?

Thank you,
[Your name]

## Email 4 — no-pressure follow-up

Subject: Quick follow-up on classroom supplies

Hi [Name],

Just a quick follow-up on the [classroom/program] supply request I shared. The priority items are still [top items], and the authorized wishlist/drop-off information is here: [link/instructions].

No pressure if now is not a fit. If you know someone else who might want the information, feel free to pass it along using the original link.

Thank you,
[Your name]

## Email 5 — thank-you/update

Subject: Thank you for supporting [classroom/program]

Hi [Name],

Thank you for helping with the [classroom/program] supply drive. Your support helped provide [items or aggregate outcome] for the start of [school year/event].

I’m keeping the update privacy-safe, so I’m not sharing student names, student photos, or private classroom details. The practical impact is simple: fewer missing supplies and a smoother start for students and teachers.

With appreciation,
[Your name]
