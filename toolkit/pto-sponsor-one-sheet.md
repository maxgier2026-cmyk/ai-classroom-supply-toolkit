# PTO / Local Sponsor One-Sheet

Purpose: give a teacher, parent, or PTO/PTA volunteer a one-page, compliant sponsor request they can adapt for a real classroom supply drive. This is not a donation-only pitch; it gives a clear supply outcome, visibility options, and privacy-safe follow-up.

## Before sending

- Confirm you are authorized to discuss the classroom, grade, school program, or PTO/PTA effort.
- Replace bracketed fields with true details only.
- Use an official wishlist, school-approved collection point, or PTO/PTA-approved process.
- Do not include student names, photos, private family details, or fake urgency.
- Check school/district policy before naming a business sponsor publicly.

## Sponsor request draft

Subject: Local classroom supply support request for [school/class/program]

Hi [business/contact name],

I am helping organize a small supply support effort for [teacher/class/program] at [school or general area]. We are trying to cover practical classroom items such as [3-5 supply examples] for [number] students.

Would [business name] be open to helping with one of these simple options?

1. **Supply basket:** donate specific items from the approved list.
2. **Wishlist support:** purchase items through the authorized wishlist link: [wishlist link].
3. **Community match:** match community supply donations up to a clear limit, such as [$ amount or item count].

If public thanks are allowed by school policy, we can share a simple thank-you note naming your business after the supplies are received. If not, we will keep the thanks private and still provide a receipt/update through the approved process.

There is no pressure either way. If this is not a fit, thank you for reading and for supporting local families in other ways.

Best,
[Name]
[Role / relationship]
[Approved contact method]

## Sponsor follow-up draft

Subject: Quick follow-up on classroom supply request

Hi [business/contact name],

Just following up once on the classroom supply request for [school/class/program]. The highest-priority needs are still [top 3 items]. If [business name] wants to help, the simplest path is [wishlist link / drop-off instructions].

If now is not a good time, no worries — I will not keep following up.

Thank you,
[Name]

## Thank-you update draft

Subject: Thank you for supporting [school/class/program]

Hi [business/contact name],

Thank you for helping with the classroom supply effort. Your support helped provide [items / supply category] for [class/program].

We are keeping student privacy protected, so this update does not include student names or private details. If public thanks are allowed, we can share: "Thank you to [business name] for helping local classrooms start the year with needed supplies."

Gratefully,
[Name]

## Privacy and compliance guardrails

- Do not claim nonprofit status unless the entity truly has it.
- Do not promise tax deductibility unless the approved recipient confirms it in writing.
- Do not pressure, shame, or repeatedly message the same business.
- Do not post student faces/names as proof.
- Do not represent yourself as school staff unless you are school staff.
