# Social Media Post Templates for School Supply Drives

Use these templates on Facebook, Nextdoor, Instagram, or your school's communication platform. Adapt freely — these are starting points, not scripts to copy blindly.

## Facebook / Nextdoor community post

**Headline:** Help stock [Classroom/Grade] at [School Name]

We're organizing a small, specific supply drive for [teacher/classroom/grade]. No broad fundraising campaign — just a clear list of what's needed and how to help.

**What we need most:**
- [Item 1] — about [quantity] needed
- [Item 2] — about [quantity] needed
- [Item 3] — about [quantity] needed

**How to help:**
[Link to wishlist OR drop-off instructions OR "message me for the wishlist link"]

Every item goes directly to the classroom. I'll post a privacy-safe update when supplies are delivered — no student names or photos, just a list of what was received.

Thank you for supporting our local kids.

## PTO/PTA announcement post

**Subject: Classroom Supply Drive — [Date Range]**

Hello [School] families —

The PTO/PTA is coordinating a targeted supply drive for [specific classrooms/teachers/grades]. We're keeping this simple:

**Priority items:**
[List 3-5 items with approximate quantities]

**Drop-off:** [Location, dates, times]
**Online wishlist:** [Link]

**Rules for this drive:**
- Please only send items from the priority list to avoid duplicates.
- No used/opened items unless the list specifically says "gently used" (e.g., board games, books).
- Label donations with the teacher/grade if you're directing them to a specific classroom.

Thank you for helping our teachers start the [semester/quarter/year] strong.

## Local business outreach post (public-facing)

We're looking for 3-5 local businesses to sponsor specific classroom supply needs at [School Name].

What we're asking: a small, one-time purchase from a specific wishlist (typical sponsorship: $25-$100 worth of supplies).

What you get: a thank-you in our PTO newsletter and a privacy-safe photo of supplies delivered to the classroom.

Message [Name] or comment below if your business would like to sponsor a classroom. Clear, simple, no ongoing commitment.

## Posting guidelines

- Post in local community groups (Facebook, Nextdoor) or school communication channels, not public/global groups.
- Tag the school PTO/PTA page if one exists and you have permission.
- Post during high-engagement windows: weekdays 7-9 AM, 12-1 PM, or 7-9 PM local time.
- Include a clear photo or graphic — posts with images get 2-3x more engagement.
- Keep student privacy: no names, no faces, no classroom numbers that identify individual kids.
- Respond to comments within a few hours. A quick "Thank you! I'll DM you the wishlist link" keeps momentum.
- Update the original post with "EDIT: [items] have been fulfilled — updated list below" rather than making new posts.
