# Start Here: Classroom Supply Support in 30 Minutes

Use this one-page route when you need a practical classroom supply support plan quickly.

## 1. Confirm permission and facts (5 minutes)

- Confirm you are allowed to share a classroom, school, PTO/PTA, or program supply request.
- Remove student names, private student details, private family details, and unapproved photos.
- Confirm the wishlist, registry, or drop-off process is real and authorized.
- Write the supply need in one sentence: `We need [items] for [class/program] by [date] because [specific classroom use].`

## 2. Build the budget (10 minutes)

Open the live calculator:

https://maxgier2026-cmyk.github.io/ai-classroom-supply-toolkit/

Record:

| Item | Quantity | Unit cost | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Example: pencils | 60 | $0.10 | $6.00 |
| Example: tissues | 4 | $2.00 | $8.00 |

Replace examples with your real list before sharing.

## 3. Pick the right message (5 minutes)

- Friends/neighbors: use a warm community message.
- Local business: use the sponsor request.
- PTO/PTA: use the coordination message.
- Follow-up: send only one no-pressure follow-up unless the platform/group rules allow more.

Template pack:

- `product/AI_CLASSROOM_SUPPLY_TOOLKIT.md`
- `toolkit/donor-email-sequence.md`
- `COMMUNITY_POST_TEMPLATES.md`

## 4. Track outcomes honestly (5 minutes)

| Person/channel | Date | Sent? | Reply | Supplies pledged | Delivered? | Thank-you sent? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | NO | | | NO | NO |

Prepared messages are not sent messages. Promises are not delivered supplies. Keep the tracker honest.

## 5. Compliance check before sending (5 minutes)

- No fake urgency.
- No pressure or guilt language.
- No student private data.
- No implication that you represent a school/nonprofit unless you are authorized.
- Follow group, platform, school, and district rules.
